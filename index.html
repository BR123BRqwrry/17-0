Player Selection Criteria

Players are selected based on peak performance during their primary decade with a franchise. The criteria:

Peak seasons We use a player's best statistical stretch with that team, not career totals.
Era-adjusted impact A 1970s rusher averaging 5.0 YPC was more dominant relative to their era than modern equivalents.
Accolades as tiebreakers All-Pro, MVP, DPOY, and HOF status break ties between similar stat lines.
Franchise association Players are listed under the team they're most identified with during that decade. Jerry Rice is a 1980s 49er, not a 2000s Raider.
Why some players are missing Players who split significant time between teams in a decade, had shortened careers due to injury, or whose peak fell between decades may not appear. This is intentional — it forces harder choices.

The simulation engine uses a non-linear formula combining EPA, Approximate Value, accolades, and hidden chemistry modifiers. Your team's rating is never shown — only the result.

How to Play

1. Spin Hit the spin button to receive a random decade.
2. Pick Choose one player from that era to fill your current roster slot.
3. No repeats Every pick must come from a different decade. You can't stack one era.
4. Simulate After 7 picks, a hidden engine simulates your 17-game season.
5. Chemistry matters Certain QB/WR combos and team stacks earn hidden bonuses.

You get 2 rerolls and 2 skips per game. Use them wisely.
