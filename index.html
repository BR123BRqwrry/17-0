Player Selection

Every notable player who spent meaningful time with a franchise during a decade is included. Selection criteria:

  • Peak seasons — Best statistical stretch with that team, not career totals.
  • Era-adjusted impact — A 1970s rusher at 5.0 YPC was more dominant relative to their era.
  • Accolades — All-Pro, MVP, DPOY, HOF break ties between similar production.
  • Franchise association — Players listed under the team they're most identified with during that decade.
  • Missing players — Players who split time between teams or whose peak fell between decades may not appear. Forces harder choices.

The simulation uses a non-linear formula combining peak stats, accolades, chemistry, and hidden modifiers. Your team rating is never shown.

17-0 Game: Build a Perfect NFL or College Football Team

The 17-0 game is a free football draft simulator where you build an all-time roster and try to achieve a perfect season. Choose NFL mode (all 32 teams, 2000s-2020s) or College Football mode (Power 4 conferences, 2020-2024). Spin for a random team, draft real players, and fill your roster. A hidden simulation engine calculates your wins.

How to Play the 17-0 Game

  1. Choose Mode — Pick NFL or College Football.
  2. Spin — Get a random team and era.
  3. Draft — Browse every real player from that team and era. Pick one.
  4. Place — Assign your pick to a roster slot (QB, RB, WR, WR, TE, EDGE, DB).
  5. Repeat — Fill your roster. You get 2 re-spins per game.
  6. Simulate — A hidden engine projects your season record.

NFL Mode: 3,000+ Real Players

NFL mode includes every notable player from all 32 teams across the 2000s, 2010s, and 2020s. Ratings are calculated from real career stats sourced from nflverse data. Draft legends like Tom Brady, Patrick Mahomes, Aaron Donald, and more.

College Football Mode: Power 4 Conferences

CFB mode features stars from the SEC, Big Ten, Big 12, and ACC from 2020-2024. Draft Heisman winners like Caleb Williams, Bryce Young, and Joe Burrow. Build your ultimate college football roster from schools like Alabama, Ohio State, Georgia, and Texas.

Hidden Rating System

The simulation engine uses position-weighted ratings, accolade bonuses (MVP, Heisman, All-American, Hall of Fame), teammate synergies, era chemistry, and a weakest-link penalty. Your team's projected record is never fully predictable — draft smart and hope for the best.

Tips to Build a 17-0 Team

  • Prioritize QB — it carries the highest position weight in the sim.
  • Look for synergy pairs like Brady + Moss, Mahomes + Kelce, or Burrow + Chase.
  • Avoid weak links — one low-rated player drags the entire team down.
  • Use your re-spins wisely if you roll a weak team/era combo.
  • Heisman winners and All-Americans get rating boosts in CFB mode.
